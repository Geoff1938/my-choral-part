Privacy & Saved Data

Stored on your device: Your preferences (voice part, recent works, playback settings) are stored locally on your device to remember your choices between visits.

Stored on our server: When you open and play a work, we record which work and movement it is, how long you play it for, an anonymous random identifier created by your browser, and your browser type (its "user agent"). This is used only to understand which works are most used and how much the player is used. We keep it for up to 5 years and then delete it. We do not record your IP address, and we do not collect any personal information such as your name or email address. Questions about this can be sent to the address under Contact Us below.

Your own files: Files you load are converted into a practice version on your device; the original file is not sent to us. When you choose Play now, we keep the practice version with its title and composer on our server, linked to a random code stored in a cookie in your browser, so you can play it again from My uploaded files. Your sets are kept the same way. Plays are recorded by title in the same way as works from our collection. You can delete files and sets at any time; files and sets that aren't used for 3 years are deleted. Clearing your browser's cookies for this site disconnects your browser from them.

Sharing: If you share a piece or set, anyone with its link can play it, and it is added to their own list when they open the link; we don't record who opens a link. Choose Stop sharing to turn a link off. To report something shared without permission, email the address under Contact Us below.

This will reset all local preferences to defaults.
